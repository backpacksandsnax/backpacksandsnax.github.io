Trail safety

Last updated 10 October 2026

Everything we share, on this site, on Instagram, and in our maps and guides, is a report of a trip we took. It is not professional guiding, medical, legal, or safety advice.

Conditions change

Trails wash out, roads close, tides turn, permits and park rules change, and weather does what it wants. A route that worked for us on the date we hiked it may not work for you on yours. Check the land manager (for example the park's own website or ranger station) for current conditions, closures, and permit rules before you go.

Know your limits

Scrambles, backcountry routes, and long remote drives carry real risk, including serious injury. Only attempt what matches your own experience, fitness, and gear. Tell someone your plan, carry the essentials, and turn around when it stops feeling right.

Every route is dated

Every route, scramble, and tide post we share says when we did it. The older it is, the more likely something has changed.

Maps and directions

Our maps open in Google Maps, which can route you onto seasonal, private, or unmaintained roads. Use common sense and follow the signs in front of you.

Park rules

No drones in national parks, in the US or Canada. Stay on open trails, give wildlife plenty of room, and pack out what you pack in.

Your trip, your call

By using our routes, maps, gear notes, or guides, you accept responsibility for your own safety and decisions. To the extent the law allows, Ben and Amy, and Backpacks & Snax, are not responsible for injury, loss, or damage that comes from following them.

Gear and links

Gear notes are our honest opinion from our own use. Follow the maker's instructions and ratings: a filter, a pair of crampons, or a heated sock only does what it's built for. When a post includes an affiliate link, free product, or paid partnership, we say so in that post.
