Privacy

Last updated 10 October 2026

Backpacks & Snax is run by Ben and Amy. This page covers backpacksandsnax.com, backpacksandsnax.shop and backpacksandsnax.club. The short version: these sites collect almost nothing about you.

What these sites do not do

  • No cookies.
  • No analytics, ad pixels, or tracking scripts.
  • No session recording or keystroke logging.
  • No sign-up forms or accounts yet.
  • Fonts and images load from our own domain, so your browser does not contact Google or any other third party to show these pages.

What our host logs

The sites are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the IP address of every visitor for security purposes, whether or not you have a GitHub account. We cannot see or control those logs. See the GitHub Pages data collection notice and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you email us

If you write to info@backpacksandsnax.com, we keep your name, email address, and message so we can reply. We don't add you to any mailing list, and we never sell or share your information. Our email is hosted by Microsoft 365 through GoDaddy.

On Instagram

We use Instagram's official tools for our own account, @backpacksandsnax, to post and to see how our posts do. That gives us counts like reach, saves, and likes, plus the comments people leave on our posts. We read comments to answer questions and to find ideas for new posts, and we reword any question we save, without your name.

We keep a list of the usernames that have commented and the date we first saw each one, so we can welcome new people. We also keep the text of comments for up to 24 months so we can spot common questions and topics, stored without usernames and with any @mentions removed. Both are deleted after 24 months. We never sell or share any of this. To have your comments and username removed from our records, email info@backpacksandsnax.com. Instagram's own privacy policy covers everything else you do on Instagram.

Links to other sites

Links to Instagram, Linktree, Google Maps, and other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies. Those apply once you leave our pages. Our map links, like backpacksandsnax.shop/map/maritime-loop/, forward you straight to Google Maps, and Google's privacy policy applies from there. Our downloadable route files, if you open them in a map app, may load standard map icons from Google.

Children

These sites are meant for adults and are not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

Wherever you live, including California and Canada, you can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email info@backpacksandsnax.com and we'll answer within 30 days. Ben is responsible for privacy at Backpacks & Snax; reach him at that address.

Changes

Before we add a newsletter, link counting or analytics, a shop checkout, or a members' area, we'll update this page to say exactly what each one collects. The date at the top always shows the latest version.
